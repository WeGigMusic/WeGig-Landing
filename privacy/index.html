Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

WeGig Limited operates the WeGig app and website and is responsible for the personal information it processes. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how you can manage it.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information, account identifiers and the gig details you save. Your chosen profile name and photo are stored on your device.
  • Location, if you grant permission, to suggest nearby gigs and venues. You can change this permission in your device settings.
  • If you choose to share app usage analytics, information such as screens and setlists viewed, device characteristics, your account identifier and email address where available. We also record your analytics preference.
  • Information you send us, such as feedback, public comments, support messages and waitlist registration details.
  • If you make an optional contribution, Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive transaction details to verify the contribution, manage supporter status and handle refunds.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the app and website.
  • To provide your account, saved gig history and other requested features.
  • To personalise your experience, including location-based recommendations.
  • To understand app usage through optional analytics, review feedback and prioritise new features.
  • To verify contributions, manage refunds and prevent fraud.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the service.
  • To communicate with you if you contact us.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Legal bases for processing

Where UK GDPR or equivalent data protection legislation applies, we process personal information on an appropriate legal basis, depending on the purpose of the processing.

  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to provide your account and the services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: Where appropriate, to maintain security, prevent fraud, respond to enquiries and improve the service, taking account of your privacy rights.
  • Consent: Where consent is required, including for optional app usage analytics. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable accounting, tax or other legal requirements.

Children and young people

You must be at least 13 years old to create a WeGig account. Users under 18 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law.

We recognise that younger users require additional privacy protections. We do not intend to collect personal information from children under 13 through WeGig accounts.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to WeGig without the necessary permission, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Location information

Location-based features are optional. If you grant location permission, WeGig uses location information to help suggest relevant gigs and venues.

You can withdraw location permission through your device settings at any time. This may affect the availability or accuracy of nearby recommendations.

App analytics

App usage analytics are disabled in the launch version of WeGig. In versions that offer optional app usage analytics, we use PostHog to understand how WeGig is used and help improve the app. We ask for your permission before enabling this optional collection.

If you agree, we may collect information about screens viewed, features used, artist pages and setlists viewed, and device and app characteristics. This information may be linked to your WeGig account identifier and email address, where available, to help us understand usage and troubleshoot issues. This information is not anonymous.

We do not enable session replay or IP-based geographic enrichment for this app analytics integration. Network requests necessarily involve an IP address; our PostHog project is configured to discard client IP data rather than retain it with analytics events.

Sharing app usage analytics is entirely optional. Declining does not restrict your access to WeGig features. If your version offers optional app usage analytics, you can change your preference at any time in Profile under Share usage analytics. We store your preference against your account, including the version of consent and the time it was last updated.

Turning analytics off stops new collection on your device. If you are offline, your updated preference may need to synchronise before it takes effect on other devices. Withdrawal does not automatically delete information previously collected with your permission or affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You can contact us to request deletion of previously collected analytics.

Our PostHog project is configured with a 12-month analytics event retention period. Account-level analytics information may be subject to separate retention and deletion processes.

These controls relate to optional app usage analytics, rather than information necessary to operate your account, maintain security or process contributions. Older app versions may not include these controls.

Website, feedback and waitlist

Feedback suggestions, comments and any optional name you provide may be displayed publicly on the WeGig website. Please do not include private or sensitive personal information in your submissions. We use this information to manage feedback and improve WeGig. You can contact us to request removal of your contributions.

The Feedback page uses browser local storage to remember votes and store a randomly generated identifier used when submitting feedback, comments or votes. This helps operate the feedback system and prevent duplicate voting. You can clear this information in your browser settings, although doing so may affect the voting functionality. Clearing browser storage does not automatically remove information already submitted to us.

We no longer use PostHog tracking on the Feedback page. Previously collected website analytics may remain subject to applicable retention and deletion processes.

Before launch, we used Google Forms for an optional waitlist. If you joined, we use the contact details you provided to send the updates you requested. You can withdraw your permission by contacting us.

When the form was available, Google may have processed technical information such as your IP address and browser information, and used cookies or similar technologies. Further information is available in Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing information

WeGig does not sell your personal data.

We use third-party service providers to support the operation of WeGig. These include providers of authentication, database storage, hosting, analytics and payment processing services.

Apple and Google process app store payments under their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the rights, security or integrity of our service and its users.

International data transfers

Some third-party service providers may process or store information outside your country of residence.

Where applicable data protection legislation requires safeguards for international transfers, appropriate legally recognised transfer mechanisms must be used.

Data storage and retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, manage your account and fulfil the purposes described in this policy.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the purpose for which it is processed and applicable legal, accounting and security requirements.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Delete your WeGig account

You can request account deletion by emailing help-wegig@proton.me. Include the email address associated with your WeGig account so we can verify the request.

If your version of WeGig has a Delete account option, you can also open Profile and select it. This clears saved profile information and gig reminders from the device you use to delete the account.

Account deletion removes your sign-in account and saved gig history. We also request deletion of analytics linked to your account. Analytics deletion is processed separately and may take longer.

We retain limited transaction records for accounting, refunds and preventing transaction reuse, and a deletion record to keep the account closed. Retained purchase records are disconnected from your WeGig account. Account deletion does not automatically refund contributions.

Your rights and choices

Depending on the applicable data protection legislation, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request a portable copy of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Make a complaint to a relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.

In the UK, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
